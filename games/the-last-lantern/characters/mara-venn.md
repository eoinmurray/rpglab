# Mara Venn

## Player

Single player.

## Concept

A municipal maintenance runner trusted to reach failing harbour infrastructure during storms and make practical decisions without waiting for headquarters.

## Background

Mara apprenticed in the dock workshops before transferring to emergency maintenance. She knows the harbour’s machinery and many of its workers, but not their private plans.

## Strengths

Electrical and mechanical repair. Moving safely through industrial spaces. Speaking plainly with working crews.

## Hindrance

Mara’s left hand is stiff from an old cable injury; delicate work performed quickly or without proper tools may suffer -2.

## Equipment

Insulated maintenance coat, compact tool roll, line launcher, hand lamp, short-range radio, voltage tester, one replacement arc contact, and a municipal access badge.

## Condition

Unhurt and rested.

## Personal goal

Keep the harbour working without helping the Harbour Office discard the people who maintain it.

## Current scene

Mara stands at the maintenance platform after returning from Beacon Seven by cable tram. The beacon is operating, the *Mercy Bell* has entered the protected channel safely, and its antibiotics are secure. Mara has reported to Ilya Chen that Senn Orra deliberately removed the relay without authorisation, creating a serious safety violation, while protesting the planned automation cuts. Ilya has recorded the account. The Beacon Seven incident is concluded.

## Private knowledge

Dispatcher Ilya Chen said Beacon Seven stopped transmitting six minutes before the briefing and resisted a remote reset. The incoming *Mercy Bell* carried refrigerated antibiotics and reached the outer shoals in thirty minutes. Mara was authorised to repair the beacon or use its manual flare. Senn arrived for a scheduled inspection, deliberately removed the relay without authorisation, and did not know the ferry had been diverted. Senn returned the relay, allowing Mara to restore the beacon. Ilya confirmed that the beacon’s identifier and transmission were clear, and Captain Pell acknowledged the signal. The *Mercy Bell* entered the protected channel safely. Senn showed Mara a Harbour Office notice stating that Beacon Seven is to be automated next week and four maintenance posts eliminated. He admitted his demonstration was poorly timed because he did not check for diverted traffic, and confirmed he was not authorised to remove the relay or create the outage. Mara reported these facts and the safety violation to Ilya, who recorded them.

## Current scene history

Mara crossed to Beacon Seven by cable tram, reported the initial situation to Chen, entered the service room, and asked Senn where he came from. Senn identified himself as the scheduled inspector and admitted removing the relay. Mara proposed reinstalling it, and Senn handed it over. Mara reseated and tested the relay, restored the beacon’s lantern and radio identifier, and reported the restored signal to Ilya. Mara told Senn the signal was working and double-checked with Ilya, who confirmed the transmission was clear and that the ferry had acknowledged it. Mara waited while the *Mercy Bell* followed the beacon safely through the outer shoals and entered the protected channel. Mara asked Senn why he removed the relay. Senn explained that he was protesting the planned automation and showed Mara the Harbour Office notice. Mara asked whether he acted without authorisation. Senn confirmed that he was authorised to inspect the station but not to remove the relay or create the outage. Mara returned to the maintenance platform by tram and gave Ilya her final report, identifying the unauthorised outage as a serious safety violation and documenting Senn’s motive and the planned cuts.
