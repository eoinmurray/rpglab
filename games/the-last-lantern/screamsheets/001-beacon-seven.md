# Beacon Seven

## Player pitch

Beacon Seven has gone dark during a storm. A medicine ferry reaches the outer shoals in
forty-five minutes. Restore safe guidance before it arrives.

## Briefing

Harbour dispatcher Ilya Chen calls Mara from the maintenance depot. Beacon Seven stopped
transmitting six minutes ago. Remote reset failed. The incoming ferry *Mercy Bell* carries
refrigerated antibiotics and cannot safely wait outside the storm wall.

Ilya authorises Mara to take the cable tram to the beacon, repair it, or activate its
manual flare. He wants a status report as soon as she reaches the tower.

## Starting situation

Rain lashes the open maintenance platform. The one-seat cable tram waits on its rail
above black water. Its rack holds a heavy emergency battery, while Mara's ordinary kit
is already secured beside the seat. Carrying the battery will slow the tram and leave no
room to work comfortably during transit, but it could power the beacon independently.

The first decision is preparation and departure, not a puzzle. Mara may take the battery,
request information or equipment, inspect the tram, contact someone, or depart directly.

## Objective and stakes

Guide the *Mercy Bell* safely through the shoals. A repaired lamp, restored radio beacon,
manual flare, improvised signal, or credible coordination with the ferry can succeed.

Failure risks the ferry aborting with its medicine, striking the shoals, or entering on
an unsafe course. State a specific danger before any action can cause serious harm.

## Hidden truth

Dock electrician Senn Orra disabled the beacon's control relay twenty minutes ago. The
Harbour Office plans to use Beacon Seven as the first unattended automated station and
eliminate four remaining maintenance posts next week. Senn intended a short outage that
would demonstrate the need for workers, but the *Mercy Bell* was diverted to this channel
after another route flooded. Senn did not know it was coming.

Senn removed the relay rather than damaging it. It remains in his satchel. He is now in
the lower service room, frightened and trying to devise a repair that will not immediately
expose him. He will not knowingly endanger the ferry.

## Cast

### Ilya Chen

The dispatcher. Brisk, responsible, and focused on the ferry. He knows the outage began
after Senn's scheduled inspection but has not accused him. He can obtain ferry updates,
authorise equipment, or send security, although security will take thirty minutes.

### Senn Orra

A veteran dock electrician facing redundancy. Defensive if treated as a saboteur, candid
if Mara acknowledges the threatened jobs, and immediately cooperative when shown that
medicine is at risk. He wants the ferry safe and the automation plan stopped or exposed.

### Captain Daro Pell

Captain of the *Mercy Bell*, available by radio once the beacon deck is reached or through
Ilya. Pell can slow, hold briefly, or follow an improvised signal, but needs clear and
credible instructions.

## Locations

### Cable tram

An exposed ten-minute crossing above rough water. A normal crossing requires no check.
Climbing or repairing something during transit is hard and risks lost equipment or delay.

### Beacon deck

A circular platform with the dark lantern, radio aerial, manual flare housing, tram rail,
and a hatch to the service room. Wind and rain make exposed delicate work difficult.

### Service room

A dry concrete chamber containing the control cabinet, maintenance terminal, spare cable,
workbench, and Senn. The cabinet shows an intact circuit with its removable relay absent.

## Leads and leverage

The terminal records Senn's badge entry and the deliberate relay removal but not his
motive. Tool marks show careful removal rather than storm damage. Senn's satchel contains
the relay. The automation notice is folded in his coat and can also be confirmed through
Ilya. Any two of conversation, records, physical inspection, or direct observation can
establish what happened; no single clue is mandatory.

## Pressure

The ferry initially has forty-five minutes. The tram crossing consumes ten. Careful work
in the service room normally consumes five. A substantial detour, failed hazardous action,
or prolonged standoff may consume another five or ten, but ordinary questions do not.

When fifteen minutes remain, Pell reports entering the outer channel and asks for guidance.
When five remain, the ferry must slow, commit to an improvised signal, or abort. Advance
these events from established fictional time, never from turn count.

## Possible developments

Senn may offer the relay in exchange for Mara reporting the outage as evidence against
automation. Ilya may order Mara to prioritise the ferry and defer blame. The storm may
damage the radio aerial after a failed exposed action. The emergency battery can restore
the lamp without the relay but must be wired on the beacon deck.

These are available reactions, not required scenes. Do not force a confrontation, expose
Senn without evidence, or require Mara to choose between saving the ferry and protecting
workers when a clever approach can address both.

## Encounters

### Exposed repair

Use an extended encounter only if Mara attempts difficult work on the storm-lashed deck.
The objective is to establish reliable guidance before conditions force retreat. Useful
states include `Secured by line`, `Tools exposed`, and `Aerial unstable`. Failure costs
time, position, or equipment before it causes harm.

## Rewards and consequences

Saving the ferry earns Mara the trust of Pell and the dock crews. Reporting Senn may
protect Mara's licence but strengthen the automation programme. Concealing the act may
protect him temporarily but leave the underlying plan intact. Documenting both the safe
repair and the dependence on skilled workers creates leverage for a public challenge.

## Completion conditions

Conclude when the *Mercy Bell* has safely entered or definitively aborted, and Mara has
decided what to report about the outage. Success, failure, compromise, exposure, and
deliberate concealment are all valid outcomes. Do not continue inventing emergencies once
those questions have durable answers.
