# Breakwater

## Player knowledge

Breakwater occupies a chain of concrete islands behind an immense storm wall. Cargo,
food, medicine, and passengers cross the flooded outer districts by rail ferry. Beacon
lanterns guide those ferries through reefs and half-submerged buildings.

Municipal maintenance runners are licensed to enter service sites, requisition common
parts, and make emergency repairs. They are competent workers, not police. Dockworkers
and municipal crews are exhausted after months of automation cuts and worsening storms.

## Factions

The Harbour Office keeps traffic moving with too little money and treats delay as the
greatest danger. The Dock Union protects skilled workers and distrusts automation. North
Channel Freight owns several ferries but depends on public beacons and municipal crews.

## Useful technology

Beacon lanterns combine an electric arc, rotating mirror, radio identifier, and manual
chemical flare. Maintenance equipment is rugged and comprehensible. Local terminals
keep timestamped fault and access records. Radios work poorly inside reinforced tunnels.

## Director-only truths

No faction is secretly omnipotent. Infrastructure failures have physical causes, records
can be incomplete without lying, and ordinary workers retain agency under pressure.
