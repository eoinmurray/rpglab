# Rules

## Character creation

This playtest uses the pregenerated character in `characters/mara-venn.md`.

## Declaring actions

Describe what Mara attempts and, when relevant, the approach or equipment she uses.
Routine movement, inspection, conversation, and competent maintenance succeed without
a roll unless immediate pressure creates an interesting consequence.

## Checks

Roll a d20 only when an action is uncertain, consequential, and has an interesting
failure. Difficulty is 10 for pressured, 14 for hard, or 18 for severe. Apply +2 for a
clearly relevant strength or preparation and -2 for a relevant hindrance. Meeting the
difficulty succeeds; exceeding it by at least 5 is a strong success. Natural 1 and 20
are critical failure and critical success.

Failure changes position, consumes time or equipment, creates danger, or forces a hard
choice. It never makes the player repeat the same action until a roll succeeds.

## Harm and recovery

Describe harm fictionally. Minor harm impairs an immediately relevant action. Serious
harm ends unsafe exertion until Mara receives help. Failure never kills Mara without a
previously stated mortal risk and a meaningful chance to avoid it.

## Equipment

Ordinary equipment works as expected. A listed item may enable an approach, avoid a
check, justify +2, or be consumed to prevent a consequence. Do not invent ammunition,
battery, or paperwork chores unless the Screamsheet establishes them as meaningful.

## Time and pressure

Pressure advances because Mara spends significant fictional time, triggers an event, or
gives another actor a credible opportunity to act. It does not advance once per message.
