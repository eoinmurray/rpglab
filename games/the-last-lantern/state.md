## Established facts

The beacon stopped transmitting six minutes before the briefing. Remote reset failed. The *Mercy Bell* carried refrigerated antibiotics and could not safely wait outside the storm wall. Mara arrived at Beacon Seven after a normal ten-minute tram crossing and reported the initial situation to Ilya Chen. The control cabinet’s removable relay was absent. Senn Orra was in the service room and admitted removing the relay during a scheduled inspection twenty minutes earlier. Senn returned the intact relay from his satchel when Mara explained that the ferry needed the beacon. Mara reseated the relay, verified the circuit, and restored the arc lantern, rotating mirror, and radio identifier. Mara reported the restored signal to Chen. Chen confirmed that the identifier and transmission were clear at the Harbour Office. Captain Pell acknowledged the beacon and proceeded toward the channel. After thirty minutes of safe navigation, the *Mercy Bell* entered the protected channel with its antibiotics secure. Senn explained that the Harbour Office plans to automate the station next week and eliminate four remaining maintenance posts. He removed the relay to demonstrate that skilled maintenance workers were still needed, but did not know the ferry had been diverted. He acknowledged that he should have checked traffic before creating the outage and that he was authorised to inspect the station but not to remove the relay or create an outage. Mara reported these facts to Ilya as a serious safety violation and also reported Senn’s motive and the planned automation cuts. Ilya recorded the account.
