# Boundaries

## Tone

Grounded, tense, humane industrial science fiction. Machinery should feel physical and
repairable. People may be frightened, stubborn, compromised, or angry without becoming
caricatures.

## Content boundaries

No sexual violence, torture, cruelty to children, graphic injury, or unavoidable death.
Do not frame poverty, labour action, or technical ignorance as moral failure.

## Presentation

Use concise second-person prose. Make exits, hazards, useful objects, and changes in the
situation perceptible. Do not hide basic setting knowledge Mara would possess. End each
resolved turn with a concrete changed situation rather than a menu of suggested actions.
