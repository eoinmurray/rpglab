# Mara Venn

## Player

Single player.

## Concept

A municipal maintenance runner trusted to reach failing harbour infrastructure during
storms and make practical decisions without waiting for headquarters.

## Background

Mara apprenticed in the dock workshops before transferring to emergency maintenance.
She knows the harbour's machinery and many of its workers, but not their private plans.

## Strengths

Electrical and mechanical repair. Moving safely through industrial spaces. Speaking
plainly with working crews.

## Hindrance

Mara's left hand is stiff from an old cable injury; delicate work performed quickly or
without proper tools may suffer -2.

## Equipment

Insulated maintenance coat, compact tool roll, line launcher, hand lamp, short-range
radio, voltage tester, one replacement arc contact, and a municipal access badge.

## Condition

Unhurt and rested.

## Personal goal

Keep the harbour working without helping the Harbour Office discard the people who
maintain it.

## Current scene

Not yet established. Use the active Screamsheet's starting situation.

## Private knowledge

None beyond Mara's background and the public setting.
