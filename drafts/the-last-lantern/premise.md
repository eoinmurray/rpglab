# The Last Lantern

## Player proposition

You are Mara Venn, a harbour maintenance runner in the storm-walled city of
Breakwater. When essential machinery fails, you reach it before the weather does.

## Intended experience

A short solo industrial adventure about practical problem-solving, investigation,
and choosing who should bear the cost of keeping a city alive. Expect one clear job,
a small location, three people with competing interests, and several viable solutions.

## Campaign loop

Receive a municipal job, prepare, enter a hazardous site, discover the human problem
inside the mechanical one, and decide what repair or compromise to leave behind.
